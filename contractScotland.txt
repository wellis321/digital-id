II.1.4) Short description

North Ayrshire Council (“NAC”) are looking for information on property exit sensors or equivalent.

NAC’s current supplier have discontinued their Property Exit Sensor which NAC regularly used for service users who are likely to leave their homes and possibly put themselves in danger.

NAC require a product that fits the following specification:

-A Property Exit Sensor which is compatible with Tunstall Digital Dispersed Alarm Units

-It should monitor the door, and movement within the house, with a secure method of arming and disarming the system

-Has configurable start, stop, and absence times

-Has an arming period to allow carers to exit the property

NAC welcomes suggestions, proposal, innovation from suppliers, by completing the questionnaire below and returning to sophieshield@north-ayrshire.gov.uk before close of business Friday 11th April 2025.
